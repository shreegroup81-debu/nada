ABOUT HIM
DANIEL CEASAR WAS BORN ASHTON SIMMONDS IN OSHAWA ONTARIO CANADA. HIS DAD WAS A GOSPEL SINGER SO HE GREW UP AROUND CHURCH MUSIC AND THAT IS WHERE HIS SOUND KIND OF STARTED FROM. YOU CAN HEAR IT IN HIS VOICE EVEN NOW. IT SOUNDS LIKE A HYMN SOMETIMES EVEN WHEN THE SONG IS ABOUT A GIRL.
HE GOT KICKED OUT OF HIS HOUSE WHEN HE WAS A TEENAGER BECAUSE HE WANTED TO DO MUSIC INSTEAD OF SCHOOL. THAT IS KIND OF CRAZY TO THINK ABOUT BECAUSE NOW HE IS A GRAMMY WINNER. HE USED TO COUCH SURF AND WORK ON MUSIC WHENEVER HE COULD. THAT STRUGGLE IS PROBABLY WHY HIS EARLY SONGS SOUND SO SAD AND REAL.
HIS FIRST BIG PROJECT WAS A MIXTAPE CALLED PILGRIMS PARADISE. IT DIDNT BLOW UP RIGHT AWAY BUT PEOPLE WHO KNEW ABOUT IT KNEW IT WAS SPECIAL. THEN HE DROPPED GET YOU WITH KALI UCHIS AND THAT SONG CHANGED EVERYTHING FOR HIM. IT WAS EVERYWHERE FOR A WHILE.
IN 2017 HE RELEASED FREUDIAN WHICH IS HIS DEBUT STUDIO ALBUM AND IT GOT NOMINATED FOR A GRAMMY. AFTER THAT HE JUST KEPT GOING WITH CASE STUDY 01 IN 2019 AND NEVER ENOUGH IN 2023. HE ALSO WON A GRAMMY FOR BEST R AND B PERFORMANCE FOR THE SONG BEST PART WITH H.E.R WHICH IS PROBABLY HIS MOST FAMOUS SONG EVER.
WHAT I LOVE ABOUT HIM IS HE DOESNT RUSH HIS MUSIC. HE TAKES YEARS BETWEEN ALBUMS BECAUSE HE ACTUALY CARES ABOUT WHAT HE PUTS OUT. HIS LYRICS ARE ABOUT LOVE AND HEARTBREAK AND GOD AND GROWING UP CONFUSED AND I THINK THAT IS WHY SO MANY PEOPLE CONNECT TO HIM SO MUCH. HE IS DEFINATELY THE GOLDEN CHILD OF THIS GENARATION OF RNB.
